Use your Android phone as a microphone for your PC

Litz sends your Android phone's microphone to a Linux or Windows computer, and can play the computer's sound on the phone. It works over your Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

Install Litz @VERSION@ Free and open source, for Linux and Windows.

Install

You need Litz on the computer and on the phone.

Linux

curl -fsSL https://github.com/ArturoAtomplay/litz-audio/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Installs into ~/.local without root and starts at login. Needs PipeWire 1.0 or newer, or PulseAudio. There are also .deb and .rpm packages and a NixOS module.

Windows

Download the installer 64-bit

To use the phone as a microphone, Windows also needs the free VB-CABLE driver. Listening to the computer on the phone works without it.

Android

Download the APK Android 6 or newer

Pairing takes a minute

  1. Put the phone and the computer on the same network.
  2. Open Litz on the phone and tap your computer.
  3. Both screens show a six-digit code. If they match, click Allow on the computer.

You do this once. After that the phone reconnects by itself whenever the computer is on.

What you get

  • The phone shows up as a normal microphone, so you can pick it in Discord, OBS, Zoom or a browser call.
  • Listen sends the computer's sound to the phone, from the output you choose.
  • Noise suppression (RNNoise or DeepFilterNet), automatic level, a noise gate and a three-band equalizer. They run on the computer, not on the phone.
  • Opus audio at 48 kHz over an encrypted connection. Only phones you have approved can connect.

Questions

How is it different from WO Mic or AudioRelay?

Litz is open source, and every feature is free. It also runs as a native Linux service for PipeWire and PulseAudio, not only on Windows.

Can I use it over a USB cable?

Yes. Plug the phone in and turn on USB tethering in Android's settings. Litz then connects through the cable.

Does it work over the internet?

No. The phone and the computer have to be on the same local network, or joined by USB tethering.

Does it need root or administrator rights?

Litz doesn't. On Linux it lives in your home folder, and the Windows installer is per-user. Only VB-CABLE asks for administrator rights.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't know it. Every release has a SHA256SUMS file if you want to check your download.

Is there an iPhone or macOS version?

Not yet. Today it is Android on one side and Linux or Windows on the other.

Litz Audio turns your phone into a microphone and a speaker for your computer. Audio travels over your own Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

You also need Litz on the computer. It runs on Linux (PipeWire or PulseAudio) and on Windows, and you can get it at https://arturoatomplay.github.io/litz-audio/

<b>Microphone</b>
The phone shows up on the computer as a normal microphone, so you can pick it in calls, recordings and games.

<b>Listen</b>
Hear the computer's sound on the phone, from the output you choose.

<b>Voice processing</b>
Noise suppression, automatic level, a noise gate and a three-band equalizer. They run on the computer, not on the phone.

<b>Pairing</b>
Tap your computer in the app and check that both screens show the same six-digit code. You do this once; after that the phone reconnects on its own, and only phones you approved can connect.

Audio is encrypted and stays on your local network. Litz has no accounts and no analytics.

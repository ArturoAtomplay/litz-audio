# Code of Conduct

Litz is a small project and I would like it to stay a pleasant place to report a bug, ask a question or send a patch.

## What I expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea or the code, not with the person.
- Assume people mean well. Many of us write in a second language, and tone gets lost.
- Stay on topic in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What is not welcome

- Insults, harassment, or comments about someone's gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, nationality, age or disability.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam and advertising.

## If something goes wrong

Mention @ArturoAtomplay in the thread, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. I may edit or delete comments, lock threads, and block accounts that keep ignoring this.

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
